## Exam bodies (not ROAC jurisdictions either)

- **ExAC** (Examination for Architects in Canada) — www.exac.ca
- **NCARB ARE** (US's Architect Registration Examination, also accepted by ROAC) — www.ncarb.org

Reminder: not all jurisdictions continue to recognize ARE results from other jurisdictions equally —
this is one of the explicit "check Appendix B" flags in the source manual.
