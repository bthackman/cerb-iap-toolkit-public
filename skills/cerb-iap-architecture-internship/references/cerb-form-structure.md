# CERB Form Structure Reference

Source: IAP Manual 4th Edition, Appendix C (instructions) and Appendix F (the form itself). This
describes the physical/digital structure of the Canadian Experience Record Book form itself, separate
from the broader process (`iap-process.md`) or category content (`experience-categories.md`).

## Form sections, in order

1. **Intern Identification** — name, address, phone.
2. **Employer Identification** — practice name/address, "Nature of Employer's Activities," Experience
   Supervisor name + position.
3. **Mentor Identification** — name, practice, phone numbers.
4. **Experience Period** — From/To dates (day/month/year), and a Full-time vs. Part-time checkbox.
5. **Role of Intern / Project narratives** — free-text section where the intern describes their
   specific activities for each project listed in the Summary of Projects. This is where the
   "demonstrate competence, not just hours" requirement lives — see note below.
6. **Summary of Projects** (up to 10 per submission period; add extra sheets if more, renumbering
   11, 12, 13...). For each project:
   - Project Name
   - Location
   - Project Type (new construction / addition / renovation / etc.)
   - Occupancy (assembly / institutional / industrial / residential / commercial / care and detention /
     mercantile / business and personal services)
   - Gross Floor Area
   - Budget
   - Number of Storeys
7. **Summary of Experience** — a grid mapping the 17 experience categories (rows) against the up-to-10
   projects (columns), with hours entered per project per category, subtotaled by category (A/B/C) and
   totaled per project.
8. **Intern Declaration** — "I declare that the enclosed information is an accurate record of my
   architectural experience." Name, signature, date.
9. **Comments and Declarations**:
   - **Employer comments** — covering 4 specific prompts (see below).
   - **Supervising Architect Declaration** — "I declare that the preceding information is an accurate
     summary of the Intern's architectural experience." Name, signature, date.
   - **Mentor Declaration** — "I declare that I have met with the Intern in accordance with IAP." Name,
     signature, date.

## The 4 employer comment prompts (Supervising Architect fills these in)

1. Comment on the level of responsibility/involvement *requested of* the intern vs. the level *actually
   taken and performed* by the intern.
2. Comment on the intern's overall attitude/philosophy/professional goals as perceived by the employer.
3. Recommendations for the intern's next ~6 months of experience.
4. Comment on the extent to which the intern was exposed to the activities outlined for each category in
   which experience was obtained.

These four prompts are a good lens for what a *strong* CERB submission narrative supports — if an
intern's project narratives don't give the Supervising Architect enough to credibly answer #1 and #4,
the submission is thinner than it should be.

## Administrative rules for filling out the form

- Record time in **hours**, no daily maximum referenced.
- If prepared **manually**: write neatly in ink; any alterations/white-outs must be initialed by the
  Supervising Architect; separate/additional pages must also be initialed by the Supervising Architect.
- If prepared **electronically**: print a hard copy and have every page initialed by the Supervising
  Architect. (Note: some jurisdictions, e.g. Ontario's OAA, have since moved to a fully digital portal
  with digital sign-off rather than print + ink initials — this is a place where practice has evolved
  past the literal 4th-edition text. If the person mentions an online portal, don't insist on the
  print-and-initial version; flag that procedures may have digitized since this manual edition and to
  follow their jurisdiction's current portal instructions.)
- Submit a hard copy bearing **original signatures** to the ROAC jurisdiction for review (subject to the
  same digitization caveat above).
- Retain a personal copy of every submission.
- Submit within **8 weeks** of the date of the last entry.
- Recognized Students should submit at the **end of each work term** rather than waiting for the
  900–1,000 hour mark.
